Facts about how we collect and share your information

This annual notice explains how Northgate Bank protects and shares information about your accounts. It applies to personal checking and savings customers.

The information we collect

  • Your name, address, Social Security number, and account balances.
  • Transaction history, payment history, and account usage.
  • Information from consumer reporting agencies when you apply for credit.

How we protect your information

We use secure systems, access controls, and employee training to protect the information you entrust to us. We do not expose your account credentials through ordinary email.

Your choices: You can limit certain sharing, opt out of marketing offers, and set account alerts. These choices do not affect your ability to use your account.

Everyday banking stays the same

This notice does not change your account terms, fees, or interest rates. No response is required.

When we share information

We may share account information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as payment processors, statement printers, and technology vendors.

Reasons we can share information

  • To process transactions: payments, deposits, and account transfers.
  • To maintain accounts: credit reporting, fraud prevention, and customer service.
  • For legal compliance: responding to court orders and regulatory requirements.
  • For our own marketing: offering relevant Northgate products and services.

Limits on sharing

Service providers must protect the information they receive and use it only for the services they perform. We do not sell account numbers or personal financial records.
