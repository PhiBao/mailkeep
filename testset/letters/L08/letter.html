Annual Notice of Changes for 2027

Your Maple Senior Health Plan coverage will continue for 2027. This notice explains the changes that take effect on January 1, 2027.

What is changing

  • The copayment for a primary care office visit changes from $20 to $22.
  • The preferred pharmacy network adds three Harbor City locations.
  • Your monthly plan premium does not change.

What stays the same

  • Your member ID and your current care team remain active.
  • Preventive care visits remain covered at no cost.
  • The Member Services phone number stays (555) 010-2200.
No action is required now. Keep this notice with your health plan records. A separate summary of benefits and provider directory will arrive before January 1.
